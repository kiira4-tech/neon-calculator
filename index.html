Frequently asked questions.

Is NeonCalc free to use?

Yes. The calculator is designed to be completely free for everyday calculations.

Can I use scientific calculations?

Yes. Turn on Scientific Mode to access trigonometric functions, logarithms, square roots and mathematical constants.
